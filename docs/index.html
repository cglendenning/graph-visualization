Install on iPhone

Open this page in Safari on the iPhone 12 mini this build was signed for. The install prompt will not appear on a desktop browser.

This replaces Constellation, it does not upgrade it. The app was renamed, so it carries a new bundle identifier and installs alongside the old one. Delete Constellation from your home screen after this finishes.

What changed in 2.2.0
  1. While you read a topic, the app quietly draws the rosette for all six satellites in the background. Tapping one is then immediate rather than a two to six second wait.
  2. Warming runs three at a time, not six, to stay well inside what a public endpoint should be asked for.
  3. A warmed draw is used once. Returning to a topic later still gives you a different six.
  4. Labels and property lists are cached for the session, bounded so a long session cannot grow without limit.
Article summaries are fetched at runtime from Wikipedia and shown verbatim under CC BY-SA 4.0, credited in the app to the article they came from. Topics and relationships come from Wikidata under CC0, a public domain dedication. Nothing under a share-alike licence is bundled into the app.
